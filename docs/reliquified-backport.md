# Reliquified integrations for Forge 1.20.1

The pack includes Relics 0.8.0.13, Twilight Forest 4.3.2508, and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks 3.16.3. The published 1.21.1 Reliquified addons use newer Relics and NeoForge APIs, so features included in this mod must work against the installed Forge versions.

## Upstream Twilight Forest 1.20.1 pull request

[Octo-Studios/reliquified-twilight-forest#13](https://github.com/Octo-Studios/reliquified-twilight-forest/pull/13) already ports the addon's Java sources and build configuration to Forge 1.20.1. As checked on 2026-10-27, it is still an open pull request, not a published 1.20.1 release. Its declared versions include Forge 47.4.0, Relics 0.8.0.13, and Twilight Forest 4.3.2508, matching this pack's major dependency versions.

The pull request is the primary technical reference for further Twilight Forest work. It still needs build and runtime validation: its `libs` directory contains 1.21 NeoForge jars, and its unchanged resources include 1.21-style `data/curios/tags/item` and singular `recipe` paths. Its recipes also use the 1.21 `result.id` field instead of the 1.20.1 `result.item` field. Some Java sources still reference 1.21-only Twilight Forest attachments (`TFDataAttachments`), so changing the build configuration alone will not make the entire PR work on 1.20.1. An isolated offline compile attempt stopped before Java compilation because the PR's Gradle Mixin plugin version was not cached locally.

Our relics below are independent items under the `myinfinitecreation` namespace, adapted against the PR's mechanics where possible. Installing a build of that addon alongside them would duplicate some effects and acquisition paths; review those prototypes before doing so.

## Implemented

- `myinfinitecreation:hydra_heart`: a Relics Curio in the `body` slot, as in PR #13. A defeated Twilight Forest hydra drops it with a 33% chance. Any positive damage to a surviving wearer can launch a fire projectile upward. On collision with a block or entity it creates a 4-block-wide burning puddle, damages and ignites non-allied creatures, and awards experience to the equipped heart. The chance, puddle damage, and lifetime scale with Relics stats. Both entities save their state and synchronize through Forge. The icon and particles use vanilla assets for now.
- `myinfinitecreation:thorn_crown`: a head Curio found in Twilight Forest hedge maze chests (10% chance per loot table roll). On each Curios tick it damages nearby non-allied creatures, can apply Relics' paralysis effect for a stat-controlled duration, and blocks cactus, sweet berry bush, and thorn damage. The 1.20.1 Relics dependency already provides paralysis.
- `myinfinitecreation:echo_glove`: a hands Curio found in Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks chest loot tables (4% chance per table roll). A melee hit can echo part of its damage onto another nearby hostile creature. Its chance, damage fraction, and radius are Relics stats. The 1.21 chain and launch upgrades are not implemented yet.
- `myinfinitecreation:invisibility_cloak`: back Curio; standing still or crouching grants Relics' Vanishing effect after a stat-controlled delay. A client mixin hides rendered armor while equipped. Found in hedge maze chests.
- `myinfinitecreation:firefly_queen`: charm Curio; charges over time and places Twilight Forest firefly blocks in dark, replaceable positions. Shows Twilight Forest firefly particles while visible. Found in Twilight Forest chests.
- `myinfinitecreation:maple_syrup_bottle`: charm Curio; consuming a Twilight Forest maze wafer temporarily boosts healing and can preserve the wafer. Found in labyrinth and stronghold chests.
- `myinfinitecreation:steel_cape`: back Curio; reduces non-bypassing damage and can retaliate with a Twilight Forest chain block. A mixin makes a successful cape chain hit use the relic's damage and apply Relics' Stun. Found in stronghold chests.
- `myinfinitecreation:minotaur_hoof`: feet Curio; sprinting builds momentum, increases speed, knockback resistance and Forge 1.20.1 step height, then damages collided enemies and reduces incoming damage at full charge. The Minoshroom drops it at 33% chance.
- `myinfinitecreation:twilight_feather`: charm Curio; rare executions of eligible weaker targets spawn a random Twilight Forest tiny bird. Found in tree cache chests.
- `myinfinitecreation:giant_glove`: hands Curio; amplifies the attributes supplied by the current main-hand item and gains experience from combat and mining. Found in troll chests.
- `myinfinitecreation:cicada_bottle`: charm Curio; hits can apply a registered infestation effect that attracts mobs. Uses Twilight Forest's cicada sound packet and model. Found in Twilight Forest chests.
- `myinfinitecreation:scaled_cloak`: back Curio; limited wall climbing and a level-five chance to evade damage while looking at the attacker. Drops from the Naga at 33%. Client movement is paired with a server-validated, rate-limited climb packet.
- `myinfinitecreation:deer_antler`: head Curio; Relics active ability carries or releases a small creature. The target, distance, and equipped item are checked on the server. Found in Twilight Forest chests.
- `myinfinitecreation:parasite_115` and `myinfinitecreation:parasite_116`: charm Curios; attacks can infect a target for delayed burst damage. A kill by the burst drops Experiment 115; an Ur-Ghast burst kill evolves the equipped parasite. The evolved form gains temporary rage from consuming Experiment 115. Stage 115 is found in Dark Tower chests.
- `myinfinitecreation:charm_backpack`: back Curio with a small NBT-backed charm inventory. It intercepts Twilight Forest 1.20.1 charm consumption and turns spent charms into five new broken-charm items that repair while stored. Found in labyrinth chests.
- `myinfinitecreation:chromatic_cloak`: back Curio that stores colored wool and consumes it for matching potion effects. The Questing Ram reward table has a 33% chance to add it. Its contents are shown in the item tooltip.
- `myinfinitecreation:goblin_nose`: head Curio that toggles a server-side ore scan, uses stored ore blocks as a filter, and shows the closest ore and its relative position in the action bar. Found in troll chests. This is a functional 1.20.1 substitute for the PR's disabled ore-particle cache.
- `myinfinitecreation:lich_crown`: head Curio dropped by the Twilight Forest Lich at 33%. Its storage accepts ten new gem items. Shielding restores Twilight Forest's 1.20.1 shield capability; necromancy summons loyal zombies; twilight launches a targeted bolt through a server-validated packet; absorption drains life at low health; frost freezes victims; fire boosts damage in warm biomes; vengeful enhances retaliation; ether blocks projectiles; carminite can reflect damage and heal; frenzy builds a short combo across distinct targets. All ten gems prevent lethal damage in the Twilight Forest. Lich scepter drops convert to the corresponding wand gems; six other gems can drop from their associated bosses at 33%.

All eighteen of the PR's Twilight Forest relic items are now registered and compile against the installed 1.20.1 dependencies. They still need in-game testing, especially the Curios inventory click event, charm-consumption mixin, parasite evolution, climb packet, Lich gem progression, and Lich bolt packet. Their item models use vanilla placeholder icons. The chest injections use explicit 10% or 2% rolls; these are not exact reproductions of the PR's Relics `LootEntry.weight(500)` rules, which also filter by dimension and biome.

## Original Castle Keeper reward

- `myinfinitecreation:castle_keeper_sigil` is an original Relics charm for the pack's Castle Keeper final boss, separate from the upstream Reliquified items. The existing `twilight_forest_final_boss:entities/castle_keeper` loot table gives one sigil per kill alongside the cube talisman and trophy. After the wearer takes nonlethal damage, Guardian Pulse grants absorption health and knocks back nearby hostile mobs. Shield strength, pulse radius, and pulse cooldown scale with Relics upgrades; a successful pulse awards relic experience. On a fatal hit, Last Stand works like a Totem of Undying: it leaves the wearer at one health, removes effects, grants regeneration, absorption, and fire resistance, and awards relic experience. Its base cooldown is 18,000 ticks (15 minutes); five Relics upgrades each reduce it by 1,200 ticks (one minute), down to 12,000 ticks (10 minutes). During this cooldown Guardian Pulse is disabled. Rescue and pulse cooldowns are stored on the wearer, and carried over on player death, so swapping or duplicating sigils cannot immediately reset them. Bypass-invulnerability damage is excluded, as with a vanilla Totem of Undying. The item uses an original pixel-art icon based on the Castle Keeper's face; in-game balance and visual testing are still needed.

## Iron's Spells relic adaptations

The remaining 23 upstream item IDs are registered under `myinfinitecreation` and have functional Forge 1.20.1 adaptations in `IronRelics.java`. They share Relics 0.8's chance, power, radius, and cooldown stats, where applicable. Iron's Spells chest tables have a 12% chance to select one of the 23 items. These odds and the generic upgrade stats are local balancing choices, not upstream's loot or level data. English and Russian descriptions describe the behavior that is actually implemented. Item models use vanilla placeholder textures; upstream models and artwork are not copied.

| Relics | Forge 1.20.1 behavior | Main difference from upstream |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bloody Voodoo Doll, Living Flesh | Mark enemies for delayed damage or damage when they flee beyond 16 blocks. | No needle entities, leech or ricochet upgrades, or Devour effect visuals. |
| Cardiac Trap, Flask of the Red Mist | Cancel fatal damage; the trap requires healing above one health before its timer expires, and the flask teleports behind the attacker. | Custom Heart Stop and Bloody Step effects are replaced by server-side health, timer, and teleport logic. |
| Cloak of the Bloody Feather, Dragon Blood Vial | Return delayed or immediate area damage after the wearer is hurt. | No needle projectiles, blood droplet, or persistent blood pool entity. |
| Dimension Key, Mirror of Transgression | Store and swap a same-dimension point; return to a same-dimension respawn point. | No linked portals, cross-dimension travel, or interrupted mirror channel. |
| Ender Bow, Shadow Claws, Slicer | Handheld weapons or active attacks: sight-targeted bow strike and teleport, mana-stealing claws and slash, and a melee combo with targeted slash. | No physical ender arrow, triple slash projectiles, or Slicer's cinematic area sequence. |
| Galaxy Devourer Diadem, Lunar Sextant, Pulsar Mantle | Brief gravitational vortex on kill, extra night damage, and low-health magical area burst. | No black-hole or comet entities, or sequential pulsar missiles. |
| Hat of Omniscience | Dormant item, as in the source addon. | No power exists upstream yet. |
| Immaterial Disperser | Can nullify Iron's Spells spell damage via its 1.20.1 `SpellDamageEvent`. | Does not interrupt spell casting or apply anti-magic to the caster. |
| Mask of Hunger, Ring of Blades, Ring of Elusiveness | Low-health life drain, periodic Wither on melee hits, and a damage-negating short teleport. | The ring of blades triggers on hits rather than missed swings; the ring of elusiveness has a cooldown instead of multiple charges. |
| Sealed Sword, Sealed Rapier, Sealed Claymore | Each equipped seal adds a timed magic follow-up to melee attacks; the claymore knocks targets back. | Summoned weapon entities, health, AI, dodge, healing, and taunt are not yet ported. |
| Sinner's Crown | Kills can summon a temporary Twilight Forest loyal zombie. | Uses a loyal zombie rather than the addon's custom dead one. |

These are playable prototypes of all remaining items, not a feature-complete copy of the 1.21.1 addon. The omitted entities, effects, visual assets, rank modifiers, and several active-ability details still need dedicated ports and in-game testing.

## Remaining work

### Hydra Heart differences from PR #13

| Area | Present implementation | Remaining difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire behavior | Projectile and puddle use the PR's chance, damage, lifetime, velocity, size, fire duration, and collision concept. Entity collisions also create puddles; experience goes to the currently equipped item. | In-game client and dedicated server testing is still needed. The entity-collision handling and experience lookup intentionally fix gaps in the PR. |
| Graphics | Vanilla flame/lava particles and an invisible renderer show the projectile and puddle. The item uses the Heart of the Sea icon. | Recreate an original heart texture, animated item metadata, ability icon, tooltip frame/star, OctoLib-style sparks, and projectile trail if matching the 1.21 visuals is desired. The PR's copyrighted assets are not copied. |
| Loot | `LivingDropsEvent` gives `myinfinitecreation:hydra_heart` and `myinfinitecreation:fire_gem` independent 33% drop chances from `twilightforest:hydra`. | The PR uses a Forge global loot modifier keyed to `twilightforest:entities/hydra`. Loot-table-based generation outside a living death event will differ. |
| Relics API | The 1.20.1 `RelicItem` supports the chance, damage, lifetime, upgrade levels, and experience used here. | PR #13 calls `getStatValue` / `spreadRelicExperience` and uses `LevelingSourcesData`, `LevelingSourceData`, `GemShape`, and `GemColor`; those classes are absent from the installed Relics 0.8.0.13. This port uses `getAbilityValue` / `addExperience`; the 1.21 gem-shaped leveling source is unavailable. The PR's `regenerative_heads` and extra head-slot code is commented out upstream and is not part of its working behavior. |
| Forge API | The fire and puddle are registered as 1.20.1 entity types; Forge spawn packets and NBT preserve damage, owner, and remaining lifetime. | The PR's direct `DamageSources.source(key, direct, owner)` call does not compile against the installed API. This port constructs a `DamageSource` from the registered `IN_FIRE` damage type instead. |

### Other Twilight Forest and Iron's Spells work

- Twilight Forest: all 18 relic classes have functional 1.20.1 adaptations. The Lich Crown uses stored gem counts in place of the newer Relics gem-shaped leveling sources; it uses Twilight Forest's `CapabilityList.SHIELDS` in place of `TFDataAttachments`. Several details still differ from the PR: the sovereign ability clamps lethal damage rather than granting Relics' unavailable Immortality effect; the lifedrain burst does not create its special scepter bonus loot or shatter visuals; and the bespoke Curio models and gem textures are not present. A direct compile probe of all PR Java sources against the pack's dependencies hit the compiler's first 100 errors, including removed Relics classes, 1.21 client classes, and missing Twilight Forest mixin targets.
- Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks: all 24 item IDs now have local prototypes, including `echo_glove`. The other 23 are summarized above. Echo Glove's chain and launch upgrades, along with the 1.21 addon's bespoke entities, rank modifiers, assets, and detailed spell integrations, remain to be ported.
- Some newly ported Twilight Forest mechanics are adapted to 1.20.1 rather than identical: Goblin Nose uses an action-bar ore readout; Scaled Cloak omits its original Curio model; Parasite evolution copies the old item NBT to the evolved stack; Relics gem-shaped leveling sources are unavailable in 0.8.0.13.
- Replace the temporary vanilla icons with original artwork and compare effects and balance in game.
- Test on a client and dedicated server with the pack's actual mod set. The current check is an offline Gradle build.
